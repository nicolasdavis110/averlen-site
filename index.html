Features

Everything you need to see clearly. Nothing you don't.

Track spending, share finances, and plan ahead in an app designed to feel calm rather than demanding.

Track spending

Every purchase, in its place.

Connect supported accounts through Plaid, import a CSV, or add purchases yourself. Averlen organizes them by category so the month makes sense at a glance.

  • Search by merchant, note, or amount
  • Spending, income, and net cash flow for any month
  • A monthly pace chart against your typical history

Understand trends

Notice what changed, without the guilt.

Trends compares each month with your own typical spending through the same day, then points to the categories that moved most — in neutral colors, never red-and-green judgment.

  • Spending over time, month to month
  • The categories behind each change
  • A monthly report you can open anytime

Share finances

Your household, on the same page.

Invite one partner to a two-person household. You'll both see the shared transactions and account information while keeping separate, private sign-ins.

  • Separate accounts, one shared picture
  • Secure invitations from inside the app
  • See household net worth together

Plan ahead

From this month to the decades ahead.

See your net worth over time and explore what's possible with planning tools for retirement and debt payoff — whenever you're ready.

  • A debt payoff simulator for extra payments
